Executive biography

Gishan Nissanka

Gishan Nissanka is an enterprise AI and digital transformation leader with 20 years of international experience helping large, complex organisations turn technology, data and AI into practical organisational capability. His career spans Vodafone, Ernst & Young, Worldline and Nscale, where he has led work across enterprise platforms, data strategy, analytics, responsible AI, operating models and workforce intelligence.

Gishan is the Founder of Nissanka AI Advisory, where he advises executive teams on AI readiness, responsible adoption and the redesign of work around human-AI collaboration. He develops practical transformation roadmaps connecting business value, governance, operating models, leadership behaviour and workforce capability.

At Worldline, he led data, analytics, workforce planning and AI transformation for more than 20,000 employees across over 50 countries. His work included establishing a People Analytics Centre of Excellence in India and leading a GenAI adoption programme that achieved full adoption across the leadership population in scope.

During seven years at EY Global, he held leadership roles across people analytics consulting, digital talent, data architecture and global transformation. He led EY Insights, a global self-service decision platform used by more than 1,000 users, alongside an AI-enabled workforce risk platform.

Earlier at Vodafone Group, he defined reporting strategy for a 91,000-employee organisation spanning 21 operating companies and led the HR technology integration for Vodafone's EUR 7 billion acquisition of Kabel Deutschland.

Gishan completed the Artificial Intelligence Programme at the University of Oxford and the Ethics of AI programme at the London School of Economics. He holds an MSc in Business Information Technology, an MBA and a Postgraduate Diploma in Marketing.
