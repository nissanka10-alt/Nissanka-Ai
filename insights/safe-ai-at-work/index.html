Using AI Safely at Work

10 Practical Questions Answered

Generative AI tools such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot and Claude can save time, improve access to information and help employees produce better first drafts. They can also introduce new risks when people use the wrong account, enter sensitive information or trust an AI-generated answer without checking it.

Safe AI adoption is not about banning the technology or producing a policy that nobody reads. It means giving employees approved tools, clear boundaries and practical guidance on where human judgement is still required.
